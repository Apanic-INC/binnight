Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 April 2026

Bin Night ("we", "our", "the app") is a bin collection reminder app for Australian households. We take your privacy seriously and collect only the minimum data needed to send you bin night reminders.

The short version: We store your street address and notification preferences so we can remind you which bins to put out. We don't track you, sell your data, or collect anything beyond what the app needs to work.

What we collect

  • Street address — used to look up your council's bin collection schedule. This is the only personal information we require.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier provided by Apple or Google so we can send you reminder notifications.
  • Notification time preference — the time of day you'd like to receive reminders (default: 6:00 PM).
  • Bins-out status — when you mark your bins as out, this is shared with other users at the same address so the whole household stays in sync.

What we don't collect

  • GPS or precise location data
  • Device information (model, OS version, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking
  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Contacts, photos, or media

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Look up your bin collection schedule from your local council's website
  • Send you push notifications reminding you which bins to put out
  • Sync bins-out status across your household

Third-party services

We use the following services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — cloud database where your address, notification preferences, and collection schedule are stored. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Expo — used to deliver push notifications to your device. Expo Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as you use the app. If you change your address within the app, your previous schedule data is replaced. If you would like your data deleted entirely, contact us and we will remove it within 30 days.

Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS. API endpoints are protected with authentication keys. Database access is restricted with row-level security policies.

Children's privacy

Bin Night is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact
